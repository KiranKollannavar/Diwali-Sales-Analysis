# 🪔 Diwali Sales Analysis Project  

## 📌 Introduction  
This project analyzes Diwali sales data to uncover insights into **customer demographics, purchasing behavior, and top-performing products/categories**.  
The goal is to help businesses **understand customer segments** and improve **targeted marketing strategies** for festivals.  

---

## 📝 Problem Statement  
During festive seasons like Diwali, e-commerce companies see a huge spike in sales.  
The business needs insights into:  
- Which customer groups spend the most?  
- Which states generate the highest sales?  
- Which product categories perform best?  
- What are the top-selling products?  

---

## ❓ Key Questions  
1. Which **age group** contributes the most to sales?  
2. Do **men or women** spend more during Diwali?  
3. Which **states** generate maximum revenue?  
4. How does **marital status** affect spending behavior?  
5. Which **occupations** drive higher sales?  
6. What are the **top 10 product categories and products** by sales?  

---

## 🗂️ Dataset  
- **File:** `Diwali Sales Data.csv`  
- **Columns:** Customer demographics (Gender, Age, State, Occupation, Marital Status), Purchase details (Amount, Product Category, Product ID).  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Libraries  
- **Python** → Data Cleaning & Analysis  
- **Pandas, NumPy** → Data manipulation  
- **Matplotlib, Seaborn** → Data visualization  
- **Jupyter Notebook** → Analysis environment  

---

## 📈 Analysis Performed  
1. **Data Cleaning**  
   - Removed null values  
   - Dropped irrelevant columns  
   - Standardized column names  

2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**  
   - Gender-wise spending behavior  
   - Age group vs. sales amount  
   - State-wise revenue contribution  
   - Marital status vs. sales  
   - Occupation trends  
   - Top product categories and products  

3. **Visualizations**  
   - Bar plots (Age group, Gender, States, Product Categories)  
   - Top 10 products by sales  

---

## 📊 Key Insights  
- **Women** spent more compared to men.  
- Age group **26-35 years** contributed the highest sales.  
- **Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka** were top states by sales.  
- **Married women (26–35 yrs)** showed higher spending tendency.  
- Most sales came from **IT, Healthcare, and Aviation** professionals.  
- **Food, Clothing, and Electronics** were the most purchased categories.  
- **Top 10 products** accounted for a major chunk of total revenue.  

---

## 📌 Conclusion  
The analysis reveals that **married women (aged 26–35)** from **Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka**, working in **IT/Healthcare/Aviation**, are the top buyers during Diwali.  
Businesses can **target this segment** with customized marketing and offers to maximize revenue.
